Climate hotspots across Howard County

Four documented places where flooding, extreme heat, or both concentrate more than the county average: each pulled from Howard County Climate Forward and the county’s open-data GIS layers. Filter by issue type, click a marker or a row, and read the sourcing before you draw conclusions about your own street.

This is a community education tool, not an official hazard determination. Boundaries here are approximate and meant to build general awareness. For a property-level flood zone lookup or permit-grade data, use the county’s own DFIRM floodplain viewer.
